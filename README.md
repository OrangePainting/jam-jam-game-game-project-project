# These pesky shells are getting in the way of your food!
Figure out how to crush them!

## About the Game
The game was made for [Game Dev Jam Jam](https://itch.io/jam/club-club-jam-jam) by a team of 5

You are a crow with a dream. Your dream: **to crack the biggest, toughest nut ever**!

Fly around the area, find out how to crack different nuts!

## Controls
Arrow keys or WASD to fly around
E to interact
P to pause
